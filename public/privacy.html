TuTak is a loyalty and payments app: you pay at partner merchants, earn points, and charge an electric vehicle. This page explains what we hold about you, why we need it, how long we keep it, and how to make us delete it.

What we collect, and why

What we do not do. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not track you across other apps or websites. There are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

Camera and notifications

The app asks for your camera only to scan a payment QR code. Frames are read on your phone to find the code and are never uploaded or stored. It asks for notifications only after you have signed in, to tell you about your own payments, points and charging sessions. Both are optional; the rest of the app works without them.

Who else sees it

  • The merchant you paid sees that payment — amount, time, and enough to identify it — because they have to be able to refund it.
  • Our payment provider processes the card. They hold the card details; we hold their token.
  • Charging network operators receive the session details for stations they run, so the session can be authorised and billed.
  • Our SMS provider receives your phone number to deliver verification codes.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it and to the extent it requires it.

A merchant never sees your other merchants, and never sees your card.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Or write to [CONTACT EMAIL] from the phone number on the account. Full instructions, including what survives and why, are on the account deletion page.

You get a 30-day grace period: the account is deactivated immediately and you can sign in during those 30 days to cancel. After that, your personal details are anonymised irreversibly — name, phone, email and device identifiers are removed.

Your financial records are not deleted, and cannot be. A payment is a record of money moving between you, a merchant and a bank. It is an accounting record that the law requires be kept, and deleting it would also erase the merchant's record of their own income. What we do instead is sever it from you: the transaction remains, your identity does not.

Your rights

  • Delete it. As above, subject to the accounting records we are required to keep.
  • Object or restrict. Tell us and we will stop, unless we are required to continue.
  • Complain. To us first, please — but you may go to the data-protection authority instead or as well.

Write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Passwords are Argon2 hashes. On your phone, your session lives in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore, which the operating system encrypts. Backups of our database are encrypted before they leave the machine that made them. Access to production data is restricted and every administrator action is recorded.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authority, promptly and specifically.

Children

TuTak is not for people under 18. We do not knowingly hold data about children. If you believe we have, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top is always the date of the version you are reading.
